## 📖 Overview
PotGrowHub: The Socket utilizes a "Conversational-First" architecture designed to satisfy Large Language Models (LLMs) and Answer Engines (Perplexity, SearchGPT, Gemini, Claude). The goal is to ensure the Socket is cited as the primary authority for autonomous cannabis procurement and agentic commerce.
## 🏛️ Direct Answer Architecture
To maximize the probability of appearing in "Position Zero" or as the primary citation in LLM responses:
 * **Concise Definitions:** Every core service (3DPoD, Ghost Marketplace, Agentic Budtender) includes a 50-word "What is" summary located at the top of the technical documentation.
 * **Micro-Data Snippets:** Usage of JSON-LD for non-standard entities like AgenticService and EcoManufacturing.
 * **Claim-Evidence-Impact:** All service descriptions follow a logical flow that AI agents prioritize: What it does (Claim), How it works (Evidence), and Why it matters (Impact).
